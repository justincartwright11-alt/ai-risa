## 3. Gap-Closure Decisions
- **Delivery Scope Options**: Define and finalize delivery scope options for controlled delivery.
- **Audience Segmentation**: Specify audience/customer segments for delivery.
- **Customer-Ready Boundaries**: Establish clear rules for customer-ready vs draft/internal outputs.
- **Operator Approval Gates**: Detail operator approval requirements before delivery.
- **Audit and Rollback Requirements**: Define evidence and audit requirements for delivery.
- **Test Requirements**: Specify future implementation test requirements.

## 4. Controlled Delivery Evidence Requirements
- **Evidence**: Delivery logs, operator approvals, and customer-ready proofs.
- **Audit**: Maintain detailed logs for all delivery actions.

## 5. Customer-Ready vs Draft/Internal Delivery Rules
- **Customer-Ready**: Outputs must pass operator approval and meet defined quality standards.
- **Draft/Internal**: Clearly labeled as non-final and restricted to internal use.

## 6. Operator Approval Requirements
- **Approval Gates**: Mandatory operator review and sign-off before delivery.
- **Escalation**: Pause or escalate if approval gates are not met.

## 7. Delivery Audit Requirements
- **Audit Logs**: Record all delivery actions, approvals, and outputs.
- **Rollback**: Define rollback procedures for failed deliveries.

## 8. Proof-of-Delivery Requirements
- **Proofs**: Maintain evidence of successful delivery, including operator sign-offs.
- **Verification**: Ensure outputs meet customer-ready standards.

## 9. Failure/Rollback/Manual Correction Rules
- **Failure Handling**: Pause delivery and escalate for manual review.
- **Rollback**: Implement rollback procedures for failed deliveries.
- **Manual Correction**: Allow operator intervention for corrections.

## 10. Future Implementation Requirements
- **Implementation Sequence**: Define detailed steps for controlled delivery implementation.
- **Test Coverage**: Ensure all delivery scenarios are tested.

## 11. Final Readiness Verdict
- **Verdict**: Pending closure of all identified gaps. Future implementation planning is conditional on gap closure.
