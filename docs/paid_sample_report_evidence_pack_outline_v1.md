# Paid Sample Report Evidence Pack Outline v1

## 1. Purpose
Define a docs-only outline for a paid sample-report evidence pack that translates locked Batch 2 validation evidence into conservative commercial messaging.

## 2. Product objective
Create a paid sample-report evidence pack that shows AI-RISA can produce structured, explainable fight-analysis outputs under controlled validation without claiming perfect prediction accuracy or production automation readiness.

## 3. Target buyer
- coaches
- fighters
- gyms
- combat-sports analysts
- media analysts
- serious fight fans
- early commercial partners

## 4. What the evidence pack proves
- Commercial readiness: CONDITIONAL_INTERNAL_READY
- Batch 2 minimum validation complete: YES
- Clean event-scoped identity: 3/3
- Structured prediction present: 3/3
- Button 3 structured prediction accepted: 3/3
- Normalization preview present: 3/3
- Structural evidence supported: 3/3
- Accuracy distribution: 2 partial, 1 hit
- Protected writes: 0
- Learning policy changed: NO
- Customer release count: 0
- Auto learning count: 0

## 5. What the evidence pack does not claim
- perfect prediction accuracy
- production launch complete
- commercial customer release ready
- automated learning approved
- full generalization across all promotions, sports, or event types

## 6. Included sample assets
- One-page validation snapshot with Batch 2 aggregate metrics
- Three-fight evidence table (Fight 1, Fight 2, Fight 3) with outcome and scoring fields
- Structured handoff and normalization explanation panel
- Safety-boundary panel (protected writes, learning policy, release counts)
- Buyer-facing limitations and claim-boundary panel
- Commercial FAQ for conservative usage and expectations

## 7. Recommended public wording
AI-RISA has completed a controlled three-fight validation batch showing repeatable structured prediction handoff, explainable scoring logic, supported structural evidence, and zero protected writes.

## 8. Prohibited public wording
- AI-RISA predicts fights perfectly
- AI-RISA is fully production launched
- AI-RISA automatically learns from results
- AI-RISA is proven across all promotions and combat sports
- AI-RISA guarantees winner, method, or round accuracy

## 9. Pricing position
Use this as a trust-building paid sample/evidence product before selling full premium reports. Suggested price band: $29 to $49.

## 10. Website placement
- Place as a dedicated trust-and-proof offer page in the commercial funnel
- Link from pricing and premium-report overview pages as validation evidence
- Keep claim boundaries visible near conversion call-to-action blocks

## 11. Buyer trust angle
Position the offer as transparent validation evidence: repeatable structure, explainable scoring logic, and clear safety boundaries, with explicit acknowledgment of mixed accuracy outcomes.

## 12. Sales risk controls
- Require conservative claims language across all page modules
- Keep limitations adjacent to performance metrics
- Ban guarantee language for winner/method/round outcomes
- Keep automation boundaries explicit (no auto-learning claim)
- Keep scope explicit (validated minimum batch, not full-market generalization)
